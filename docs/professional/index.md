# Professional agentic science

Scientific discipline changes how agents are used. Delegation becomes useful when its scope, evidence and stopping conditions are explicit.

## Working notes

- A scoped contract specifies editable files, acceptance checks and a deviation report.
- Reproduce a known result before claiming improvement.
- Investigate surprisingly good numbers before trusting them.
- Trace claims back to inputs, scripts and artifacts.
- Calibrate reviewers and verify their findings independently.
- Use durable project state and explicit approval boundaries.

Source: course evidence synthesis, §3. These are selected observations, not general performance guarantees.

## Three modes

| Casual | Professional | Professional-agentic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask and accept | Hypothesise, test, validate | Delegate bounded tests and preserve the evidence |
| Conversation as record | Research notebook | Durable briefs, ledgers and review tracking |
