# Middle loop: project and paper

Experiments become an argument through review and revision. A capable review step is useful only when findings lead to verified changes.

## Working notes

- Provenance audits connect paper claims to runs and datasets.
- Review findings do not reliably survive the transition into a revised draft.
- Judge calibration needs known outcomes and negative controls.
- Git and document-editor synchronisation remain operational work.
- Draft quality and reviewer scores need separate validation.

Source: course evidence synthesis, §5. These are selected observations, not general performance guarantees.
