# Verification before showing

## Evidence and reading flow

- [ ] Confirm paper identity, authors, venue/preprint, year and version. Full text, relevant captions and tables were read; otherwise show and report the abstract-only warning and mark unsupported sections unavailable.
- [ ] Keep an evidence ledger with exact values, units, uncertainty, direction, dataset/split, method, setting and source locations. Every reported number, including comparators and metadata, is backed by linked Table/Fig/section provenance, with full locations in the ledger. Verify anchors or PDF page destinations, not just landing pages.
- [ ] Label every calculation or rounding, cite inputs and state the method. Keep source settings distinct. Source links for axis scales sit with the chart; ticks are coordinates, not measurements.
- [ ] Prose cards have at most one end-of-sentence tag per sentence: class `src`, 11px, muted, superscript-style, no brackets. Tables/charts have one linked caption line such as “Source: Table 1”, no per-row tags. Only cells/marks from a different source get individual tags.
- [ ] TL;DR has exactly three sentences and at most 60 words total: problem, method, result. TL;DR and verdict have no tags; all their claims are supported elsewhere. Verdict is at most 30 words. Reader's verdict is one line, labelled agent's reading, and assesses both genuine novelty and persuasiveness.
- [ ] The problem has two to three sentences, one concrete physical failure example and the key jargon term defined plainly.
- [ ] Mechanism schematic shows actual objects and relationships, not a word flowchart. A faithful labelled data structure is the fallback. Two to four callouts appear on the drawing, with a source and accessible title/description. It occupies at least half the page width on wide screens.
- [ ] Schematic has clean geometry, consistent spacing and at most about 10 primary objects. No arrows, labels or leaders cross edges or each other. Callouts occupy empty margins with thin leaders; local/global structure is separated by layout. Dense connections are representative, with “each X connects to all Y” stated.
- [ ] Main result chart comes from the ledger, never memory. Every plotted value matches the ledger exactly, and positions match those values. Include the method and two to five baselines on one to three comparable metrics, or explicitly explain missing evidence.
- [ ] Axes use `niceTicks(min, max, n≈3–4)` and round 1–2–5 tick steps, outward-extended domains, light baselines and tick gridlines; no raw scale coordinates such as 0.0966. Retain per-panel scales when ranges differ and show “scales differ by panel” once beside the chart.
- [ ] Chart uses labelled units/scales, consistent row order, small multiples and one shared direction note. Method is accent blue, baselines grey-blue. Values at marks use a shared source caption; bars start at zero, or dots have an explicit scale. Explain differing metric directions and missing values.
- [ ] One sentence directly below the chart states what it shows, without generalising beyond the evidence. At most two big numbers on the whole page, each with a comparator, setting and provenance for both values.
- [ ] No chart value is repeated as a key number. The key-numbers slot holds another main claim or is removed. A cost/time/memory/scaling or other selling point absent from the main chart has a second small chart using the same scaffold rather than isolated big numbers.
- [ ] Every main-comparison metric where the method loses is plotted. Prefer value-labelled paired dots over bars for two metrics sharing a panel; use separate panels for differing units/scales. Retain the shared direction note, for example “lower is better”.
- [ ] Key equation or scaling uses HTML sub/sup, defines symbols, cites the paper and includes a supported cost comparison, or explicitly says it is not stated.
- [ ] How it works has three to five one-line steps faithful to the method. Setup covers datasets/sizes, splits, model sizes, compute/hardware, each sourced or marked “not stated”.
- [ ] Where it sits has three to five sourced methods with approach, cost and comparison, or explains insufficient source coverage. Mark indirect comparisons and unstated costs.
- [ ] How strong is the evidence covers three to four main claims and what was actually run. Each strong/moderate/weak rating has a four-word reason justified by evidence, and ratings are labelled as our reading. Include stated limitations with citations and label additional limitations “our reading”.
- [ ] Reproduce covers code/link/licence, data links/sizes, checkpoints yes/no/not stated, rough compute (reported or explicitly estimated) and one gotcha if found. Mark missing information; verify links without inferring availability.
- [ ] If we wanted to test it contains two to three experiments labelled “our proposals”, each with an intervention/comparison and figure of merit.
- [ ] Glossary has three to six one-line definitions; every term is actually used on the page.

## Layout and delivery

- [ ] DOM order: title/TL;DR/verdict; problem/mechanism; chart/equation and numbers; how it works/setup; positioning table; evidence drawer; reproduce/proposals; glossary fallback. Two columns on wide screens with wider schematic and chart, full-width tables, two final cards and a glossary fallback details; one column below 900px.
- [ ] At 1280x720 the title, full TL;DR/verdict and at least the top of problem/mechanism are visible. Aim for about 2,400px total height at 1280px width, roughly three screens. Cut low-value text before shrinking type; order content by importance within the fixed reading order. No huge empty areas.
- [ ] Body is 15 to 16px, tables 14px, content is at least 13px including scaled SVG text; only `.src` provenance tags are 11px, with schematic callouts allowed at 12px at 420px. Headings are bold accent or small caps. Narrow-screen charts stay legible without page-wide overflow.
- [ ] At 420px, the mechanism uses viewBox, width 100%, height auto and no fixed/minimum pixel width. Verify no horizontal page scrolling. Callouts are at least 12px; otherwise hide SVG callouts and their leaders and show the same text as a numbered list below the drawing.
- [ ] When a screenshot helper or browser exists, rendering is mandatory: capture the 1280x720 first screen and a full-page image at 1280px width for BOTH Full and #poster modes, open all images, inspect crossing schematic lines, repeated chart/key numbers and per-row source tags, and fix them along with overlaps, clipped text, unreadable labels and empty space. A clipped 2200px viewport is not a full-page capture. Re-render after fixes. Also inspect below 900px. If unavailable or blocked from launching, explicitly report that visual rendering was not checked and why.
- [ ] Replace all `{{...}}` placeholders in the finished super-poster, including SVG titles, labels, coordinates and links. Fill chart JSON using the ledger; generate the static fallback table from it and populate the empty mechanism SVG. Remove scaffold instructions and unused repeated rows/panels; give duplicated SVG panels unique accessible IDs. Escape inserted HTML text and attributes. Placeholder content remains only in the reusable template.
- [ ] Use British spelling and no em dashes. Keep the source/check-before-citing footer.
- [ ] Preserve system fonts and all six palette colours: surface `#fcfcfb`, text `#1f1f1d`, muted `#6b6a64`, accent `#2a78d6`, baseline `#9aa7b8`, grid `#e6e5e0`.
- [ ] Inline CSS/SVG, exactly one vanilla executable inline script and one inert JSON script. No inline event-handler attributes, libraries, eval or external assets. Inspect URLs, `src`, `href`, `url(`, `@import`, `<script`, `<iframe`, `<link` and protocol-relative references. Only source/code/licence/data/checkpoint navigation links may fetch remotely when followed; the SVG namespace is not a request.
- [ ] Load offline and confirm the complete page renders without network requests. Verify relative local PDF paths from the output directory. Disclose inaccessible or unverified navigation links.
- [ ] Save to the requested path or `./posters/<short-name>.html`. Report path, up to three key numbers and locations, render status and verification gaps. Keep agent time under 15 minutes.

## Progressive enhancement

- [ ] Header has title, authors, Paper/Code/Data links (or availability notices), and Poster (1 min) / Full (3 min) buttons. Full is default; `#poster` works on load and hash change. Body class/CSS implement depth. Poster shows only identity/navigation, TL;DR (no verdict), a reduced-height mechanism and the main chart’s first metric in the All systems view, retaining sources, scale/direction notes and access warnings. Everything else is hidden. Switching back to Full restores its filters and all metrics remain available.
- [ ] Full is about 2,400px at 1280px with details open. Poster fits a 1280x720 screen: via the screenshot helper measure `document.body.scrollHeight` in #poster at 1280px width and require ≤760px; record the measured height, inspect the screenshot and re-render after fixes. Keep all systems/baselines in Poster and all required evidence and losing metrics in Full. Check both modes below 900px.
- [ ] Disable JS: all substantive content is readable, all details start open, all mechanism elements and text steps are visible, the full static table is present. Controls are progressive enhancements; the fallback is hidden only after chart success.
- [ ] JSON parses; exact value/err/unit strings and sources match the ledger. Static table is generated from that JSON and matches every row, including missing values. No independent hand-entered chart dataset; no demo data rendered.
- [ ] Chart filters work by metric and system (All or one); default includes losing metrics. Panels separate settings/scales/units, row order is stable, method accent and dot labels are correct. Tooltip on hover AND keyboard focus includes value ± err unit and source (or explicit missing uncertainty). Marks navigate to verified sources; shared caption remains.
- [ ] Three to five step buttons match How it works, highlight the correct data-step elements and show a one-line caption. Other elements dim only in JS. Play advances every 2.5 s; Stop/manual selection stops it; Show all restores all elements.
- [ ] Optional scaling card exists only for a stated claim. Slider is log N with labelled normalised illustrative curves: “illustrative, from the stated complexity, not the paper's data”. Measured dots have sources and compatible normalisation; calculations/inputs are disclosed. Static text preserves claims and measurements.
- [ ] Every evidence row is a details element: claim | strength summary; actual tests, verbatim supporting quotes with linked sections, and what was not tested inside. Preserve qualifications and distinguish our reading from paper statements.
- [ ] Inline dfn terms have tabindex="0" and data-def, definitions appear on hover/focus, with three to six fallback definitions in end details. No separate glossary card.
- [ ] Setup, Where it sits, Reproduce and If we wanted to test it use details open in Full and hide in Poster. Buttons are native button elements, controls labelled, aria-pressed correct and focus visible.
- [ ] Inspect executable script for network/dynamic code: grep for `fetch|XMLHttpRequest|import`, and inspect WebSocket/EventSource/sendBeacon, URL assignment and DOM resource creation. No script network requests or eval; source navigation only follows user action. No external assets.
- [ ] Template and delivered HTML parse; executable JS passes syntax checking. Report render and JS-off checks separately. Screenshots cannot verify hover/click behaviour: try all controls and keyboard interaction in a browser and report any untested behaviour.
