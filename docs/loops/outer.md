# Outer loop: programme and career

The infrastructure exists, but you currently have to build it. Durable state and fleet management support programmes without proving an end-to-end career system.

## Working notes

- Persistent briefs and handoffs survive session loss.
- Fan-out inventories work when responsibilities and merge steps are clear.
- Approval gates keep costly or irreversible choices with the responsible human.
- Session dispatch and self-healing bridges have production evidence.
- The corpus has little evidence for multi-person physics-programme governance or career decisions.

Source: course evidence synthesis, §6. These are selected observations, not general performance guarantees.

!!! note "Maturity, in Oct 2026"
    Generic infrastructure is comparatively mature and already operational. Physics-programme-specific practice is the larger evidence gap; a working fleet is not a complete programme.
