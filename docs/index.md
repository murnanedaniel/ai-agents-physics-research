# AI Agents in Practice for Physics Research

KIT · 22 October 2026 · 09:00–17:00 · Daniel Murnane. A draft companion for particle and astroparticle PhD students and postdocs, across theory and experiment.

## Working notes

- Show what agentic science can achieve today.
- Explain the machinery and confront the limits.
- Make professional scientific practice the centre.
- Separate research loops from implementation choices.
- Work through experiments, papers and programmes with live demos.
