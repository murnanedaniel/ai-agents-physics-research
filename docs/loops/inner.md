## Working notes

- Baseline gates and negative controls recur across independent projects.
- A surprising result triggers diagnosis rather than celebration.
- Artifact completion matters more than a process staying alive.
- Small pilots must be long enough for the suspected failure to occur.
- Diagnosis and recovery were stronger than unsupervised tuning.

Source: course evidence synthesis, §4. These are selected observations, not general performance guarantees.
